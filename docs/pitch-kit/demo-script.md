# 3-minute demo script

For a workshop owner, on the laptop (or tablet), with the owner's own phone in their hand. It follows the spec's four moments, then the project. The lines in Albanian are suggestions; say them in your own words. The demo bar at the top of the laptop has every moment as one button (1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · +).

## Before the meeting (10 minutes, see the rebrand checklist)
- The app is rebranded with their logo and name, locally and online, and **Rivendos demon** was pressed today (laptop) and `pnpm deploy:demo` (or the online reset) was run.
- On the laptop: signed in with **Hyr si {Pronari}**. Light mode unless the room is dark.
- Your own phone scanned the QR once today (the online page opens).
- In the meeting, ask first: *"A mund t'jua dërgoj një mesazh provë në WhatsApp?"* Only with a yes, open **Më shumë… → Numri i takimit**, type their number and save. (It stays in this browser for 12 hours; "Hiqe" removes it.)

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Sot
Show the Sot screen. Point at the dark card.
> "Kjo është punëtoria juaj në një ekran: kush ju ka borxh, sot kush shkon ku, dhe paratë që i ka montuesi në xhep."

## 0:20 – 1:00 · Moment 1: Borxhet ("Kush më ka borxh?")
Tap **1 Borxhet**.
- The total at the top, the late ones first, sorted by amount or by days late.
- Point at **Shpend Berisha**: the green **Kujtesë** shows only where a reminder makes sense now (late, no pause, nothing sent in 7 days).
- Point at **Bekim Rexhepi** (cousin, "Mos sugjero kujtesa") and **Valbona** (reminded 3 days ago): no green button.
> "Asgjë nuk dërgohet vetë. Programi e përgatit mesazhin e sjellshëm, ju vendosni a e dërgoni."

Tap Shpend's **Kujtesë** to show the prepared text, then go back (don't send).

## 1:00 – 1:40 · Moment 2: Faqja e klientit (QR)
Tap **2 Klienti**. The QR for Besa Zeka appears.
> "Skanojeni me kamerën e telefonit tuaj."

On their phone: their logo, "Porosia juaj është në punë", **Montimi · Nesër · paradite**, the three steps, WhatsApp and call buttons. No prices, no login.
> "Kur klienti pyet 'kur e sjellni?', i dërgoni këtë link një herë. Djali në Gjermani e sheh në gjermanisht."

Optional: choose **Liridona Shala · Deutsch** in the same sheet and scan again.

## 1:40 – 2:20 · Moment 3: Dërgoja Dritonit
Tap **3 WhatsApp** ("Dërgoja Dritonit punët e nesërme").
- The two jobs of tomorrow in one message: client, phone, place, directions, product, **Mblidh €…**.
- With the meeting number set, **Dërgo te: Numri i takimit** is chosen. Tap **Dërgo me WhatsApp**, review the text in WhatsApp and send it to their phone.
> "Kështu i merr montuesi punët në mbrëmje. Pa aplikacion, pa thirrje. Ju sapo e morët si të ishit Dritoni."

Back in the app, **U dërgua? → Po** writes it in the order's history.

## 2:20 – 2:50 · Moment 4: Driton telefonon "Mora €800"
Tap **4 Pagesa €800**: Fatmir Krasniqi's order, installation today, **€800** left.
1. **Montimi u krye**
2. **Po, të gjitha €800** (Te Driton is already chosen)
3. **Ruaj**
> "Klienti ka paguar, nuk i kërkohet më kurrë. Por €800 janë te Dritoni derisa t'i sjellë: këtu poshtë 'Para te montuesit'."

Tap the alert's **Konfirmo** only if you want to show the handover.

## 2:50 – 3:00 · + Projekti (doors, floors, furniture companies, builders)
Tap **+ Projekti**: "24 kuzhina · Kompleksi Arbëria" (Lumi: "100 dyer · Banesat Kodra").
> "Ndërtuesi paguan vetëm për atë që është montuar. Programi e di sa ju ka borxh sot."

## Close
> "Ditën e parë i fusim borxhet nga fletorja juaj, në 20 minuta. Pastaj zyra e mban."

Hand over the one-page offer.

## If something goes wrong
- **No internet for the phone**: tap **Parapamje këtu** in the QR sheet and show the page on the laptop.
- **WhatsApp won't open**: use **Parapamje në telefon (demo)** on the send screen.
- **Something was changed by accident**: **Më shumë… → Rivendos demon** (15 seconds).
- **Want to show late debts move**: **Data e demos +7 ditë**, then **Sot**.
